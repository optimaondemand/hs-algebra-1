In today's lesson, we will be exploring how to create the equation of a quadratic function that is represented by a table of values. In Algebra 1, a pivotal skill is translating a table of values into the equation of a quadratic function. This lesson will guide you through identifying critical points, like the vertex or zeros, and another point from the table. We'll learn how to use these points to construct the quadratic function's equation.

Before we dive into examples, remember:

  • The vertex is the highest or lowest point of the parabola, crucial for finding the equation in vertex form .
