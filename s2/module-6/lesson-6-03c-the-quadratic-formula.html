In today’s lesson, we will explore how to solve quadratic equations using the quadratic formula. This method allows us to find solutions to any quadratic equation, even those that cannot be factored easily. Building on your understanding of factoring and completing the square, the quadratic formula provides a reliable way to solve equations when other methods are less practical. By the end of this lesson, you will be able to apply the quadratic formula to find solutions to quadratic equations, including those with irrational solutions (solutions that are not exact whole numbers or fractions).
